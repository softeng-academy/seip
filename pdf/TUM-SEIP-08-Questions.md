AF-12.5: Rich-Client Aspects
============================

-  BLOOM: 5: Evaluate
-  TYPE:  Trade-off
-  KIND:  free answer

discussed. Proposal A: to stay completely independent of external dependencies, the team
develops its own small in-house Libraries for Interface Widgets, Data Conversion, and Client
Networking, and picks a separate Open Source Library for each of the other Aspects. Proposal B:
the team builds on a few Open Source Frameworks (e.g., Bootstrap, VueJS, and ComponentJS) and
adds a few Libraries for the Aspects not covered by them. Evaluate both proposals and recommend
one.
